# SOC 2 & the IBM i

Map CC6/CC7 Trust Services criteria to profiles, object authority, journal, and detect/respond on the green screen.

A finding should cite the command/screen evidence (profile, object authority, journal) and state impact in plain language stakeholders understand.

SOC 2 CC6.1 / CC7.2: privileged access and detect/respond on the green screen, not in a slide deck.

SOC 2 CC8.1: patch and change management — WRKPTFGRP cumulative/HIPER levels and DSPPTF cover letters are partition evidence, not spreadsheet assertions.

Audit operations on the green screen span change management (PTF), journal retention, and authority ownership — use the IBM i Audit Operations pack for the full spine.

Findings need severity plus decision impact — who must act and by when.
